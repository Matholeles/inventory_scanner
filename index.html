How to import into Excel
Step 1 — Export or send the CSV
Go to the Log tab and tap Export CSV to download it, or enter an email address and tap Send CSV to email it straight to your PC.
Step 2 — Transfer the file to your PC
If you used Send CSV, just open the email on your PC. Otherwise, any one of these works:
  • Email it to yourself as an attachment
  • Save it to Google Drive / OneDrive / Dropbox
  • Connect tablet via USB and copy the file across
  • WhatsApp it to yourself
Step 3 — Open in Excel
Double-click the .csv file — Excel opens it automatically. If it doesn't look right, go to Data → From Text/CSV and import it manually.
Step 4 — Apply the changes to your master tracker
The CSV has columns: Timestamp, Item ID, Quantity, Direction.

Use Excel's VLOOKUP or XLOOKUP to match Item IDs from the CSV against your master tracker and update the In/Out columns accordingly. Or paste the CSV data into a new sheet and update manually.
⚠ Important
Tap Clear log on the Log tab after you have imported the CSV into Excel. This prevents the same entries from being counted twice next time you export. After a successful send, the app offers to clear the sent entries for you.
